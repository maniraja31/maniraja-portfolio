Smart Budget Visualization & Analytics for government financial data

A web-based platform designed to simplify and visualize government financial data through interactive dashboards and analytical tools. The system enables users to explore budget allocations, revenue, expenditure, fund utilization, and financial trends through dynamic charts, filters, and reports. It also provides role-based access, financial data management, and real-time data retrieval.

Technologies:

Python,HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript,Firebase Firestore, Chart.js, Plotly.js.

Key Features:

Interactive financial dashboards Budget allocation and tracking Revenue and expenditure analysis Department-wise and year-wise comparison Real-time data management Reports and analytics Role-based authentication
